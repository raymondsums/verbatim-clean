Senior Product Designer

Northwind · San Francisco, CA (Hybrid) · Full time

Northwind builds route planning software for delivery fleets. Dispatchers use it to plan the day, and drivers use our mobile app to follow the route, scan packages, and report problems from the road. We are a team of forty, and design sits close to engineering.

As a Senior Product Designer you will own the driver app end to end, from research with drivers in the depot to shipping in weekly releases. You will design for people who are moving, glancing at a phone mounted on the dash, and working in bad light and bad signal.

You have shipped mobile products used by people on the job. You prototype in code or with AI tools when a static mockup will not answer the question. You write clearly, and you are comfortable presenting tradeoffs to engineers and to the founders.

We care about safety first, fewer taps, and screens that work in a two second glance. We measure success by on time deliveries and how rarely drivers call dispatch for help.

Salary range $170,000 to $205,000. Hybrid, three days a week in our San Francisco office.
