# Voice Samples

Optional. Paste a few things you wrote yourself, with no AI help, to a
real reader: an email, a post, a README. One "##" section each, with the
text as a quote block. Drafts copy how these sound, never their facts.

## Example: a short post

> I spent a week watching people pay for things on their phones. Most of
> them gave up at the same screen, so we took that screen out. Nothing
> clever. We just stopped asking for the same address twice.
