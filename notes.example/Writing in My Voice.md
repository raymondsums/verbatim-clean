# Writing in My Voice

Optional. Drafts follow this note, and it outranks Verbatim's built-in
style rules. Write it in your own words. Delete anything that is not true
for you.

## How I write

- Short sentences. I say what a thing is and what it does.
- I use contractions. I'm, I've, it's.
- I name the real numbers and the real product, not "a key initiative".

## Words I never use

leverage, passionate, seamless, synergy, spearheaded

## Watch for

- Starting with "I am excited to".
- Ending a paragraph on a moral.
