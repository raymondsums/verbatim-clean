# Story Bank

Real stories from your master file and the answers you sent. Each one has
the situation, what you did, the result, and where those lines came from.
Drafts can be told to use one. Built by Verbatim; safe to edit by hand.

The story below is an invented example that shows the format. Delete it
once you have your own. The Answers page can also build stories for you
from content/master.md and the answers you have sent.

## Checkout redesign

**Situation:** Half the people who started checkout on the mobile app left before paying.

**What I did:** I watched ten customers try to pay, then cut the form from three screens to one and moved the address lookup up front.

**Result:** Checkout completion went from 48% to 71% in the first month.

Sources:
- Master file: Cut the checkout form from three screens to one.
- Master file: Checkout completion went from 48% to 71%.
