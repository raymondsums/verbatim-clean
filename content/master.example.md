# Master content

Everything the tailoring step writes from. It reorders and rewords what is
here; it never invents. So this file is the product, not the code: a thin
file makes plausible generic prose, a detailed one makes a real resume.

Write it by hand from your own resume, portfolio and notes. Keep numbers
exact and mark the version you consider canonical when two sources
disagree.

## Summary

One paragraph on who you are and the work you want.

## Experience

### Job Title, Company (Jun 2018 - Jun 2026)

- What you did, with the number that made it matter. "Cut checkout
  from three screens to one; completion went from 48% to 71%" beats
  "improved conversion".
- One bullet per outcome, not per task.

#### Project Name

What the problem was, what you designed, what changed as a result. Detail
here is what lets the tailoring pick the right project for a given job.

## Education

School, degree, years.

## Skills

The tools and practices you would defend in an interview.
